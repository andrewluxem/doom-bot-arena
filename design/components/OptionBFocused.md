# Option B · Focused state

Focusing a bot swaps its feed into the centre where the map was and moves the map to a pinned bottom-right cell; the flanks keep all eight positions so any bot is one click away.

- The focused bot's flank tile turns into an `ON STAGE ↗` placeholder framed in its colour, so the ring keeps its spatial order.
- Tethers are hidden while focused; they return with the map.
- Compact telemetry (4-column grid with the `Return to Multiview [ESC]` action in its header) sits left of the pinned map.
