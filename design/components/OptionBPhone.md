# Option B · Phone

On a phone the map stays the hero at full width and the feeds become a horizontal strip ordered by sector, starting at the Central Hall duel and running clockwise.

- The strip opens scrolled to the hottest sector; swipe for the rest. Sector chips (`C·W`, `NE`, `SE`…) replace the desktop tethers.
- Shown with the standings drawer open (full 8-row table) and the timeline docked at its foot.
- This is the one deliberate deviation from the pinned-corner rule: the map is the anchor here. It pins bottom-right only when a bot is focused (the stage takes the hero slot), as on desktop.
